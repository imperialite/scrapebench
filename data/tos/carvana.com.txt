B. Card Payments to Carvana When You Sell Us a Vehicle
If you are selling your vehicle to Carvana and utilize a card to pay Carvana for any negative equity, we may place a temporary card hold (a pre-authorization) for the amount you owe when you submit your card information in checkout, and later charge your card when your sale is completed.
A hold only confirms your card can cover the balance, it is not a charge. A hold reduces the available balance or credit in your payment account and remains in place until your sale is completed or canceled, or until it expires (up to 7 days), whichever happens first. While a hold is in place, those funds will not be available for your use.
You can cancel your appointment any time before your sale is completed. If you cancel, we will promptly release any hold, but your bank controls how quickly it drops off your account.
If your appointment moves beyond a hold window, you authorize Carvana to re-authorize the hold (by cancelling the prior hold and placing a new one) so the hold stays current.
If the amount you owe increases, we will ask you to approve a new hold.
