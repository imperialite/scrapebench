Program Description

The purpose of the Card Link Program is to provide registered users with the ability to earn money in the form of credits towards retail gasoline purchases (“GasBack”) by making qualifying purchases from participating merchants, which qualifying purchase opportunities will be described in the GasBuddy mobile app and website from time to time. At its sole discretion, GasBuddy and participating merchants define what purchases are eligible for GasBack and the related GasBack reward associated with such qualifying purchases as a percentage of the total qualifying purchase. In order to receive GasBack from us for qualifying purchases of goods or services, you must link at least one eligible credit or debit card (each, a “Payment Card”) (i) through the GasBuddy mobile app, or (ii) as part of your application and the resulting issuance of a GasBuddy™ Mastercard® Credit Card (where you will automatically be enrolled in the Card Link Program unless you elect to opt out of the Card Link Program). Eligible cards are listed in Section 4 below. GasBack is redeemable in one of three ways. First, you can redeem GasBack through your Pay with GasBuddy card and apply GasBack directly to gas purchases made with your Pay with GasBuddy card. Second, you can redeem GasBack through your GasBuddy™ Mastercard® Credit Card where GasBack will be automatically be applied directly to gas purchases made with your GasBuddy™ Mastercard® Credit Card.  Third, if you do not have a Pay with GasBuddy account or a GasBuddy™ Mastercard® Credit Card account, you are eligible to redeem GasBack through GasBuddy’s Transfer GasBack Funds Program. Under the Transfer GasBack Funds Program, registered users who have earned GasBack rewards, but who do not have a Pay With GasBuddy account or a GasBuddy™ Mastercard® Credit Card account, are given the ability to (i) redeem all (and not less than all) of their available GasBack balance for select digital gift cards, or (ii) waive their right to redeem all (and not less than all) of their available GasBack balance and instead instruct GasBuddy to make a charitable donation in the amount of the waived GasBack balance to a charity or charities as may be designated by GasBuddy from time to time. In order to participate in the Transfer GasBack Funds Program your GasBack rewards balance must be at least Five Dollars ($5.00). You can click here for a detailed description of eligibility and other terms related to GasBuddy’s Transfer GasBack Funds Program.

Important note: GasBack® expires 12 months from the date that it posts in your GasBack Account.

Effective as of 11:59:59 ET on December 31, 2021, all GasBack that is 12 months or older (that is, GasBack posted to your account on or before December 31, 2020), will expire and may no longer be redeemed pursuant to these Terms or any other terms and conditions related to GasBack.

A linked Payment Card means that the holder of the Payment Card has provided their Payment Card number and expiration date under the "Savings Settings" section of the GasBuddy app. Once this has been done, the holder of the linked Payment Card can earn GasBack at any participating merchant in the Rewards Network Program.

GasBack Credits

GasBack credits will not appear or be reflected on your transaction receipt from the Merchant at the time of purchase, and will instead be reflected in the savings tab of the GasBuddy app. GasBuddy will use commercially reasonable efforts to display GasBack transactions in the app in a timely manner. GasBack will show as pending in your transaction history until it becomes available for redemption which takes on average 5-7 business days from the date of your transaction.
