Client Referral Program
Empower offers a cash reward in the amount of a $100 account credit for Empower Premier Clients (“Referring Client”) who refer their friends and family (“Referred Client”) to become an Empower Premier My Total Retirement Client. When a Referred Client elects to utilize Empower’s My Total Retirement managed account service in their funded Empower Premier IRA or Premier Investment Account ($50,000 account minimum requirement) for a period of at least 90 days, both the Referring Client and the Referred Client will receive a cash reward of $100 which will be paid to the Client’s Premier Investment Account or, if the Client does not have a Premier Investment Account, to the Client’s Premier IRA.  The cash reward will be paid within 90 days after the above conditions are met.
The maximum cash reward paid to a Client in any twelve consecutive month period will not exceed $500.00.  The value of the cash reward may constitute taxable income to the Client.  Empower will reflect any cash reward paid to the Client’s Premier IRA as an IRA contribution for the year in which the reward is paid to the IRA.  Please consult your tax advisor.  Empower does not provide legal or tax advice.
Clients will not pay any additional or increased My Total Retirement managed account fees as a result of participating in the Client Referral Program.
In referring a Referred Client to My Total Retirement, the Referring Client agrees that participation in the Client Referral Program will conform to the terms contained herein as well as the provisions of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, 15 U.S.C. § 80b-1 et seq., and the rules thereunder, Title 17, Part 275 of the Code of Federal Regulations (the “Investment Advisers Act”).
