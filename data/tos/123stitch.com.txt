Our Commitment To Privacy
Your privacy is important to us. We provide this notice to explain our online information practices and the choices you can make about the way your information is collected and used. To make this notice easy to find, we provide a link to it at the bottom of every page on our website.
The Information We Collect
This notice applies to all information collected or submitted on the 123Stitch.com website. On some pages, you can order products, make requests, and register to receive materials. The types of personal information collected at these pages are:
Name
Address
Email address
Phone number
Payment information
On some pages, you can submit information about other people. For example, if you order a gift online and want it sent directly to the recipient, you will need to submit the recipient's address. In this circumstance, the types of personal information collected are:
Name
Address
Email address
Phone number
To monitor the security and performance of our website, we may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit. We do not use this information to track individual visitors, nor do we share it with any outside parties.
How We Use Information
We use the information you provide when placing an order only to complete that order. We do not share this information with outside parties except to the extent necessary to complete that order. For example, we will share the name and address you provide with our shipping partners to ship your order, and we will use your email address to notify you about delivery.
You can subscribe to receive our email newsletter as well as updates on our new products and services. Information you submit to us will not be used for this purpose unless you specifically request to be subscribed.
Information you share with us via phone, email, or live chat is used to enable our customer service team to respond to your inquiries and fulfill your requests. We do not use the information you share for any other purpose, nor do we share it with any outside parties, except to the extent necessary to respond to your inquiries.
Finally, we never use or share the personally identifiable information provided to us in ways unrelated to the ones described above without also providing you an opportunity to opt-out or otherwise prohibit such unrelated uses.
Our Commitment To Data Security
To prevent unauthorized access, maintain data accuracy, and ensure the correct use of information, we have put in place appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect online.
How You Can Access Or Correct Your Information
You can access all your personally identifiable information that we collect online and maintain by emailing us at info@123stitch.com. We use this procedure to better safeguard your information.
You can correct factual errors in your personally identifiable information by sending us a request that credibly shows error.
To protect your privacy and security, we will also take reasonable steps to verify your identity before granting access or making corrections.
Our Partners
To protect against fraud, this website is protected by reCAPTCHA. The Google
Privacy Policy
and
Terms of Service
apply.
We partner with Chatwoot to provide our live chat service. The Chatwoot
Privacy Policy
applies to information you share in conjunction with that service.
How To Contact Us
Should you have other questions or concerns about these privacy policies, please send us an email at info@123stitch.com or call us at +1-801-495-0908.
