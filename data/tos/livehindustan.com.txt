What Are Caches and Cookies?
Caches and cookies are like little notes your web browser stores when you visit websites so your browser can remember it the next time you visit those websites again.
Your browser tends to save these files. Even if they are relatively small, they will accumulate over time. It could cause performance and privacy issues on your browser.

Click the menu button ⋮ > More Tools > Clear browsing data.
On the Clear browsing data window, select which time range you want to clear.
