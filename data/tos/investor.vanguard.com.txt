Vanguard is pleased to offer a subscription text message service to provide you with text message alerts when certain transactions and clerical changes are made in your Vanguard account, as well as when you schedule or reschedule appointments with a Vanguard representative. Please note that message frequency for the Service will vary depending on the types of accounts you have, your account settings, your account and appointment activity, and your alert selections.
When will I receive account activity alerts and reminders?
Go to
My Accounts
and
Account activity alerts and reminders
under
Profile & account settings
. For Plan accounts, go to
My Profile
and
Text Message Service
. Here, you'll select the types of alerts and reminders you want to receive. Once you've selected a particular transaction type or clerical change, you'll receive, on an ongoing basis, a text message alert every time that transaction type or clerical change is requested for your eligible account registration(s). If you select appointment alerts, you'll receive, on an ongoing basis, a text message alert whenever you schedule or reschedule an appointment with a Vanguard representative, as well as a reminder text message two to three days before the appointment.  Please see the
Account activity alerts and reminders
section of
Profile & account settings
for a list of account registration types, account roles, and transactions that are not eligible for the Service.
What charges apply?
Vanguard doesn't have a separate charge for this Service; however, message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier. Participation in the Service is subject to your carrier's standard messaging rates (no premium content). By providing your consent to participate in this Service, you approve any such charges from your mobile carrier.
What if I have questions or need help?
If you have any questions or need help with this Service, please text HELP or HLP to 82393, or call 877-662-7447. For account specific inquiries, please call 877-662-7447.
What if I don't want to receive any more text messages?
To unsubscribe from this Service, you may text STOP, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, or END to 82393. Additionally, you can call Vanguard at 877-662-7447 and an associate can unsubscribe you from the Service. You may also return to the
Account activity alerts and reminders
section and deselect ALL alert types for which you have selected to receive text messages. By deselecting all alert types for which you have selected to receive text messages, you will be unsubscribed from the Service. By enrolling in this Service, you agree that following any request to unsubscribe, you will receive one final text message from Vanguard confirming that you have been unsubscribed from the Service.
What if I change carriers or mobile phone numbers?
If you change carriers, even if your mobile phone number stays the same, or if Vanguard is notified by a carrier that you have changed your mobile phone number, you may be unsubscribed from the Service. To continue the Service, you will need to complete the sign-up process again. If you update your mobile phone number through the
Account activity alerts
and reminders section, Vanguard will send a verification code to the updated mobile phone number you provide. You will then need to enter that verification code in order to maintain your subscription.
What will happen if I don't request any transactions or activities eligible for the Service for a period of time?
If you don't request any transactions or activities eligible for the Service for 18 months or more, you may be automatically unenrolled from the Service and/or sent one final text message indicating that your participation in the Service will be terminated. To continue the Service, you may need to complete the sign-up process.
